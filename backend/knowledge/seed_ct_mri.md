## Adenocarcinoma
Source: Prototype seed note (unverified, replace with a cited guideline)
What: A common type of lung cancer, often seen on chest CT as a nodule or mass near the edge of the lung.
Matters: CT appearance alone cannot confirm the cancer type; tissue confirmation is needed.
Next: Specialist review and biopsy are usually considered.

## Large cell carcinoma
Source: Prototype seed note (unverified, replace with a cited guideline)
What: A type of lung cancer that often appears on chest CT as a large mass, frequently near the edge of the lung.
Matters: CT appearance alone cannot confirm the cancer type; tissue confirmation is needed.
Next: Specialist review, staging and biopsy are usually considered.

## Squamous cell carcinoma
Source: Prototype seed note (unverified, replace with a cited guideline)
What: A type of lung cancer that often arises near the large airways and may appear on chest CT as a central mass, sometimes with a cavity.
Matters: CT appearance alone cannot confirm the cancer type; tissue confirmation is needed.
Next: Specialist review, staging and biopsy are usually considered.

## Glioma
Source: Prototype seed note (unverified, replace with a cited guideline)
What: A tumour arising from the supporting cells of the brain; on MRI it often appears as an irregular mass with surrounding swelling.
Matters: Behaviour and grade vary widely and need specialist assessment.
Next: Neurosurgical and neuro-oncology review with further imaging is usually considered.

## Meningioma
Source: Prototype seed note (unverified, replace with a cited guideline)
What: A tumour arising from the membranes covering the brain, often seen on MRI as a well-defined mass attached to the brain’s outer lining.
Matters: Often slow-growing; management depends on size, location and symptoms.
Next: Specialist review; follow-up imaging or treatment planning is considered.

## Pituitary tumor
Source: Prototype seed note (unverified, replace with a cited guideline)
What: A growth in the pituitary gland at the base of the brain, seen on MRI in or just above the sella turcica.
Matters: May affect hormones or vision, depending on size.
Next: Hormone and vision assessment and specialist review are usually considered.
